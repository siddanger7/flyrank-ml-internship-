Notebook → live endpoint, on a GPU budget

I built and deployed a RAG system under a hard GPU-credit budget on Lightning AI. First requests against a cold instance were slow enough to time out — so I added a warm-up health check and retry-backoff in the FastAPI layer, then published the before/after latency numbers instead of just claiming it works.

The failure story

What broke: first requests against a cold instance were slow enough to time out before the model was ready. The dashboard said uptime was fine; the first real user paid for it.

What I changed: a /healthz warm-up path that pre-loads model + index; retry with exponential backoff in the FastAPI layer; latency-percentile logging (p50/p95).

What it cost: a small warm-up credit cost in exchange for ~[78]% cut in p95 first-request latency. Deployment discipline is measured at the tail and under a budget — not on a warm benchmark.

Honest limits — what this isn't

  • Designed for [N] concurrent users; above that, retries do the work, not scale.
  • Retrieval quality depends on the index; this write-up measures latency, not answer quality.
  • GPU credits are capped — long idle time means a cold restart, by design.
