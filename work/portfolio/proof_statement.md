# Proof Statement — AI n-able Yourself (Week 1)

> I ship ML systems end-to-end, not just notebooks — FastAPI to GPU server to
> production. I'm proving this to a technical co-founder at a seed-stage AI
> startup who needs to de-risk whether an ML hire will actually ship a feature
> this quarter instead of running notebooks, so they'll book a 15-minute call
> to see a live endpoint.

## Why this needs to exist (the honest line)

A CV says "built ML systems." It cannot show that I carried a model from a
notebook to a live endpoint real users can hit, that deployments actually ran,
and that I know where the system breaks. This portfolio proves the shipping
part — with working endpoints, deployed projects, and honest write-ups of what
survived production and what didn't.

## The narrowing (how we got here)

- Started broad: RAG, object detection, two internships, an email-assistant SaaS.
- Cut to one claim: "I ship ML systems end-to-end, not just notebooks"
  (engineering/deployment) — because FastAPI backends, Railway deploys, and a
  Lightning AI GPU server are real shipped evidence, and the claim survives
  contact with a stranger who sees one artifact.
- One person: a **technical co-founder** at a seed-stage AI startup. Not a Head
  of Engineering — a co-founder's fear is runway and not having wasted six
  months, which a shipping ML hire directly de-risks. The HoE's concern (team
  velocity) is broader than one hire and less binary.
- One action: **book a 15-minute call to see a live endpoint.**
