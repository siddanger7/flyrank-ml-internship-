> I ship ML systems end-to-end, not just notebooks — proven by a RAG system
> deployed under a hard GPU-credit budget on Lightning AI, where I diagnosed and
> fixed a p95 cold-start spike with a warm-up health check and retry-backoff
> logic in the FastAPI layer, documenting the before/after latency numbers rather
> than just claiming it works; this is built for the technical co-founder at a
> seed-stage AI startup who has a research scientist but no one to carry models
> to production, whose real fear is burning runway on a hire who can't ship —
> and the portfolio's only job is to get that person to book 15 minutes to watch
> me walk a model from notebook to a live running endpoint, in front of them.

## The three proof anchors (claim only counts once these exist)

1. **Case-study page under `/work`** — diagram (FastAPI → GPU server → endpoint),
   the p50/p95 before/after cold-start table, and the failure story (what broke,
   what I changed, what it cost). Readable in under 2 minutes.
2. **Other projects demoted** to a one-line "other work" list (sid.ai, Email
   Assistant SaaS, Object Detection, internships) — links only, no case studies,
   no hero space.
3. **Calendar CTA directly under the case study** — "Book 15 minutes to watch a
   model go notebook → live endpoint," not buried in a footer.

## The narrowing (how we got here)

- Started broad: RAG, object detection, two internships, an email-assistant SaaS.
- Cut to one claim: "I ship ML systems end-to-end, not just notebooks"
  (engineering/deployment) — evidenced by FastAPI, Lightning AI GPU server,
  Railway deploys, and a real production failure (cold-start p95) I diagnosed
  and fixed.
- One person: a **technical co-founder** at a seed-stage AI startup with a
  research scientist but no one to carry models to production. Not a Head of
  Engineering — a co-founder's fear is runway (not having wasted six months),
  which a shipping ML hire directly de-risks. The HoE's concern (team velocity)
  is broader than one hire and less binary.
- One action: **book 15 minutes to watch the notebook→endpoint walk live.**

## Why this needs to exist (the honest line)

A CV lists skills. It cannot show that I carried a model from a notebook to a
running endpoint, kept it alive under a real budget, and know its limits. This
portfolio proves the shipping part — with the failure write-up that makes it
believable rather than a demo.
